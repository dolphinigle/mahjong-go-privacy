# Mahjong One — privacy policy

This repository exists only to serve the privacy policy for the Android game
**Mahjong One** (`com.mahjonggo.game`) over HTTPS, which Google Play requires of
every app.

The policy is [index.md](index.md), published at
<https://dolphinigle.github.io/mahjong-go-privacy/>.

The URL keeps the old name on purpose. It is the address filed with Play and
printed inside the app, and a privacy policy link that 404s is worse than one
whose slug is out of date. The app was renamed from *Mahjong Go* on
2026-09-04; the application id `com.mahjonggo.game` could not follow it either,
being permanent once published.

The game itself is a separate, private repository. Nothing here is part of it.

The short version: we operate no server and receive nothing about you — your
game, your scores and your settings never leave the device. The app does carry
Google AdMob, which shows one advertisement per app open and collects data of
its own; the policy says what, and links Google's.
